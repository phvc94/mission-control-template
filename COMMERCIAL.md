# Free use and commercial agreements

The Mission Control Template has public code under its own license.
The applicable text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

| Situation | Condition |
|---|---|
| Install it to organize your own agents and projects | Free |
| Use it internally in your company, without offering the dashboard to customers | Free |
| Adapt it for personal or internal use | Free |
| Share a copy free of charge under the same terms | Permitted, preserving the license and authorship |
| Sell the template or an adapted version | Prior commercial agreement |
| Charge a subscription or offer a hosted dashboard to third parties | Prior commercial agreement |
| Charge a customer for deploying, customizing, operating, maintaining or supporting the dashboard | Prior commercial agreement |
| Use the dashboard internally to track activities that generate revenue | Free; the activity's revenue does not by itself give rise to a commission |

## How to request commercial authorization

Contact the maintainer, [phvc94](https://github.com/phvc94).
You can open a request in this repository stating only your commercial
interest and a general description of the product or service you intend to offer.

Do not put confidential data, customer documents, credentials, revenue figures
or detailed proposals in public issues. Arrange a private channel with the
maintainer to negotiate amounts and other commercial details.

The agreement must be concluded in writing before commercialization and must define:

- The authorized product or service, the parties involved and the scope.
- The commission, revenue share or other agreed compensation.
- The revenue taken into account, the accepted deductions and the calculation basis.
- Assessment, reporting, due dates and payment method.
- Term, support and termination conditions.

This document does not set a percentage and does not replace the commercial agreement.
Sharing code publicly does not grant automatic commercial authorization.
The licenses of third-party components remain preserved.
